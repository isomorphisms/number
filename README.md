# number

Notes and experiments in algebraic number theory, arithmetic topology, and arithmetic geometry.

## First source: Billy Woods

Billy Woods's video course **An unorthodox introduction to algebraic number theory** is the first source for this repository.

- Course page: https://billywoods.net/ANT.html
- Teaching page: https://billywoods.net/teaching.html
- YouTube playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSibAQEfLnTwq2-zCB-t9v2WvnnVKd0wn
- Notes: [notes/billy-woods-ant.md](notes/billy-woods-ant.md)

Woods explicitly builds the course around motivation, examples, diagrams, and geometric intuition rather than a conventional theorem-proof sequence. That makes it useful here not only as a number-theory source but as a source of geometric models worth rendering or formalizing.

A particularly good organizing picture is **ideal arithmetic as lattice geometry**: Woods illustrates least common multiples of ideals as intersections of lattices. That bridge between arithmetic and geometry should be preserved rather than flattened into symbolic algebra.


## Arithmetic topology: Masanori Morishita

Masanori Morishita's **Knots and Primes: An Introduction to Arithmetic Topology** is a central reference for the topology/arithmetic side of this repository.

- Second edition (2024): https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-99-9255-3
- Notes: [notes/morishita-knots-and-primes.md](notes/morishita-knots-and-primes.md)

Its organizing dictionary compares knots with prime ideals, 3-manifolds with number rings, fundamental groups with Galois groups, linking invariants with residue symbols, Alexander theory with Iwasawa theory, and topological torsion with arithmetic torsion data. The second edition also adds idelic class field theory and arithmetic/topological Dijkgraaf–Witten theory.

## Scope

This repository may collect material around

- rings of integers and factorization;
- ideals and ideal arithmetic;
- Euclidean, principal, and Dedekind domains;
- units, norms, ramification, and decomposition;
- ideal class groups;
- number fields and their embeddings;
- lattices arising from arithmetic;
- local and (p)-adic viewpoints;
- arithmetic topology and arithmetic geometry.
